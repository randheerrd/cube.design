·Craft Bar

The bar every screen clears

This is the design review checklist. It is deliberately concrete — a standard you can fail. Run it yourself before review; arriving with it unchecked wastes everyone's hour.

Structure & type

Most "it feels off" reactions resolve to a hierarchy problem.

The squint test passes

Blur your eyes. The most important thing on screen is still the most prominent thing.

Type comes from the scale

No one-off sizes. If the scale genuinely lacks a step, that is a system change, not a local override.

Alignment is intentional

Optical alignment beats mathematical where they disagree. Nothing is one or two pixels off by accident.

Line length is 45–75 characters

Applies to any real prose. Table cells and labels are exempt.

Spacing is on the scale, and consistent

Related things are closer than unrelated things — proximity is doing hierarchy work.

Colour & contrast

Colour carries meaning here, so it cannot also be decoration.

Text contrast meets WCAG AA

4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text. Checked, not eyeballed.

Colour is never the only signal

Status, direction and severity carry a shape, an icon or a label alongside the hue.

The accent is used once

One accent per screen, on the thing that matters most. Two accents means neither reads.

Both themes checked

Light and dark, on the real screen — not assumed from tokens.

Red and green are not load-bearing

Roughly one in twelve men cannot separate them. Up and down need more than hue.

The four states

Principle 08, expanded. This is the most common review failure by a distance.

Empty — and first-run is separate

"Nothing yet, here is how to start" and "nothing matched your filters" are different screens.

Loading at three durations

200ms: nothing. 2s: skeleton matching the real layout. 20s: explain and offer an exit.

Error is scoped and actionable

One failed widget fails alone. The message says what the user can do, in their words.

Overflow is designed

10,000 rows, a 90-character product name, a negative number, a null. All four, tested.

Stale data is visible

If the pipeline last ran nine hours ago, the screen says so where the numbers are.

Data display

We are a data product. This section is our home turf and gets no slack.

The chart type matches the question

Comparison, composition, distribution, trend, relationship — each has a right answer. Pick from the question.

Numbers are tabular-lining and right-aligned

Columns of figures must be scannable down the column. Consistent decimal places.

Precision matches confidence

No four decimal places on an estimate. Round to the precision the number actually has.

Forecast is visually distinct from actual

The same treatment everywhere in the product, so users learn it once.

Axes are honest

Truncated axes are labelled as truncated. No dual axes without a very good reason.

Units and currency are explicit

Once per column or axis, not once per page.

Motion

Motion explains change. Motion that decorates is motion that costs.

Every transition explains a relationship

Where something came from, where it went, what caused it. If it explains nothing, cut it.

Durations are 150–400ms

Interface feedback near 150ms, entrances near 250ms, larger spatial moves up to 400ms.

Easing is from the set

Standard ease-out for entrances, ease-in for exits. No linear on anything spatial.

prefers-reduced-motion is honoured

Tested, not assumed. Reduced motion means no movement — a fade is fine.

Words

Principle 07 in checklist form.

No lorem, no placeholders, no schema names

Real copy or the screen is not ready for review.

Buttons name their verb

"Approve order", not "Submit". The label says what will happen.

Sentence case throughout

Headings, labels, buttons. Title Case only for proper nouns.

Errors are in second person and actionable

"We couldn't reach your Amazon account — reconnect it in Settings." Never a stack trace.

Numbers in copy are formatted the same as in the UI

Same separators, same rounding, same currency treatment.

Accessibility & performance

Non-negotiable, and cheap if designed in rather than retrofitted.

Full keyboard path

Every action reachable without a mouse. Tab order follows visual order.

Focus is always visible

A real focus style, designed, not the default outline removed and never replaced.

Targets are at least 44px on touch

Including the ones in dense tables.

Meaningful images have alt text; decorative ones have empty alt

Charts need a text alternative or an adjacent data table.

The design has a performance budget

Know the row count, the image weight and the query cost the design implies — before it is built.

Known contrast exception

Black on the #ff5100 accent measures about 4.1:1 — under AA for small text. It is used once, at 12px on a 52px pill, as a deliberate brand call inherited from the spec. If you introduce a second use of the accent on text, it has to clear AA properly.
