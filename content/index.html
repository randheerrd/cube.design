/ 01 — Philosophy

beliefs

What we believe

/01

We design decisions, not dashboards

The unit of value is a person changing their mind — not a chart.

/02

Confidence is a design material

When the machine is guessing, the interface says so, and says how hard.

2Priyanka — the one we get asked about most
/03

Density is respect

Experts want more per screen arranged well, not less arranged prettily.

/04

The interface shrinks as trust grows

Day one needs the explanation. Month nine needs the shortcut.

/05

We design the recovery, not the happy path

A flow is not designed until someone can get out of it.

/06

Latency is a design problem

We design the screen at 200ms, at 2s and at 20s.

/ The short version

We would rather show less and be trusted than show more and be checked.
