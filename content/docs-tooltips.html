Components

Tooltip

Stable Last updated Oct 2026

A Standard Tooltip gives a short line of extra text; a Trendline Tooltip is a small dark card with the exact numbers behind a chart mark. The Specifications tab lists every value.

Guidelines Specifications Code (not in Storybook yet) Accessibility (coming soon)

At a glance

A tooltip answers “what is this value?” for one mark on a chart, a row in a table, a slice of a pie or a region on a map, without the user leaving the dashboard. There are two kinds.

Standard Tooltip

One or two lines of plain text. Labels an icon, explains a flag or spells out an abbreviation.

Trendline Tooltip

A small dark card with real numbers: a value, a comparison period and a change. Built from a header, a table and a footer.

Not a tooltip

Something to click or fill in? Use a Popup or a Right overlay. A note that must stay visible? Use a Message.

Which one do I use?

Ask whether the tooltip carries words or numbers.

SituationUse
Label an icon-only button or explain a termStandard Tooltip, fit content
Explain a flag in two lines, with a next stepStandard Tooltip, fixed width, with header and action
Show the exact number behind a bar, point or sliceTrendline Tooltip
A common dashboard case: platforms, SLA, RCA, geographyA Trendline example rather than a custom card
A table that needs scrolling, or a formNot a tooltip: a Right overlay or a Popup
The only place a number appearsNot a tooltip alone: also show it in a legend, export or detail view

Do’s and don’ts

Seven rules in three groups. The first three come from the Figma usage guidelines.

Choosing

Do

Excludes returns and cancellations

Keep hover tooltips to one short sentence: a label, hint or definition.

Don’t

Revenue is calculated after discounts and excludes returns and cancelled orders. To change the calculation, go to Settings → Metrics, select Revenue and pick a new formula. Changes apply to all dashboards within 24 hours.

Don’t put paragraphs or instructions in a tooltip. Move long content into the page or a popover.

Click to expand

Make anything clickable inside a tooltip also reachable from the anchor itself, by keyboard and touch.

Don’t

Click to see localities

Don’t hide an action behind hover only. Keyboard and touch users can’t reach it.

Writing content

  • Numbers — Lakh (L) and Crore (Cr): “3.2 Cr”, not “32,000,000”. Percentages have one decimal place.
  • Deltas — always lead with + or −, in green for a gain and red for a loss. Never colour without the sign.
  • Header — the title is the date the numbers describe; the sub-text is the comparison period (“vs Last Day”). Keep both on one line.
  • Footer — a short italic note, such as a data-freshness caveat, never a second data point. A footer button is one clear next step (“View SKU”).
  • Standard Tooltip — a phrase, not a paragraph. No full stop on a single phrase.

Behavior & accessibility

TopicWhat to do
StatesHidden by default; visible on hover of the anchor; dismissed when the pointer leaves or on Esc.
KeyboardA Standard Tooltip also opens when its anchor gets keyboard focus.
Placement8px from the trigger or cursor, never covering the mark it describes. Flip to the other side before running off screen; keep 16px from the bottom edge.
Semanticsrole="tooltip", linked to the anchor with aria-describedby.
Interactive contentA real tooltip can’t contain anything focusable (WCAG 1.4.13). If it has a button or “Click to expand”, it is a small hover panel: the same action must also work by focusing the anchor and pressing Enter, and by tap.
Not the only sourceEvery number in a tooltip must also be reachable without hovering — a legend, an export or a detail view.

Every dimension, colour and type value is on the Specifications tab.
