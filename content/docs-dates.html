Date Selector & Calendar

A popover for picking a single date or a date range. Quick presets (MTD, 1M, 3M…) cover the common cases; a Custom option opens a calendar for anything else. Built from five parts: the Date-Selection-modal, its preset bar and Custom trigger, the Calendar and the Date-cell.

Cube Core

Storybook Figma
Guidelines Specifications Code (opens Storybook in a new tab) Accessibility (coming soon)

At a glance

Try it: switch between Date Range and Single Date, pick a preset, or open Custom and click two days. Hovering between the clicks previews the range.

A working Date-Selection-modal. Esc closes the calendar.

1 · Preset

MTD, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y for ranges; SDLW, SDLM to compare one day. Applies at once.

2 · Custom

Opens the calendar: two months for a range, one for a single date.

3 · Pick

Click 1 sets the start, click 2 the end. The trigger shows the value.

Common layouts

Open the selector from a trigger in the page header or filter bar. Offer presets first — most people pick a common period — and keep Custom for specific dates. Use Date Range for trends and totals; Single Date for day-level comparisons.

Do’s and don’ts

Do

MTD1M3M6M1YCustom

Offer presets for the common periods, with Custom for everything else.

Don’t

Custom

Don’t make people open a calendar for common ranges like “last month”.

Do

Use two months (Date Range) when picking a range — ranges often cross months.

Don’t

Don’t use the single-month calendar for ranges.

Do

3 Apr’26 - 16 Apr’26

Show the applied value in the trigger so the current period is always visible.

Don’t

Custom

Don’t leave the trigger showing “Custom” after a range is applied.

Do

242526272829

Disable dates with no data (e.g. future days) so they can’t be picked.

Don’t

242526272829

Don’t let people pick dates that will return empty results.

Do

Switch to Single Date when someone needs one day — it shows one month and day-level presets.

Don’t

Don’t make people pick the same day twice in Date Range mode to select a single date.

Writing content

  • Date format — D MMM’YY: “3 Apr’26”. Ranges join with a spaced hyphen: “3 Apr’26 - 16 Apr’26”. The apostrophe is the typographic ’.
  • Month label — short month and 4-digit year: “Apr 2026”.
  • Placeholders — “Start Date - End Date” for a range, “Select Date” for a single date. Replace them with the value as soon as a date is picked.
  • Presets — keep the short codes (MTD, 1M, SDLW…) and explain them in a tooltip where people may not know them.

Behavior & accessibility

TopicWhat to do
Week startMonday. The weekday row is M T W T F S S.
Range pickingClick 1 = start, click 2 = end. Hovering between the clicks previews the range. Clicking before the start swaps start and end.
PresetsApply immediately and close the popover. Confirm the exact date maths with product.
LimitsDays outside the min / max use Disabled, and the nav buttons disable at the limit.
CloseEsc or a click outside closes it and discards an unfinished range (start only).
KeyboardEvery preset, the trigger, the nav buttons and each day are buttons in tab order; Enter / Space selects.
Screen readersEach day’s accessible name is the full date (“Fri Apr 03 2026”); selected days carry aria-selected; the month label is announced when it changes.
