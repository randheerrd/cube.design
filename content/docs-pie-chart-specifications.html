Pie chart

A donut that shows how one total splits into a few parts (2–6): share of platforms, a rating split, where a loss came from. An optional centre label carries the total, and a legend sits beside or below the ring.

Behavior & accessibility

The Figma documentation for Pie chart has no API frame. These rules follow the Bar chart so the two charts behave alike; confirm them with engineering before building.

TopicWhat to do
OrderSegments run clockwise from 12 o’clock, largest first; the legend lists them in the same order.
Small partsNo more than 6 segments. Group the rest as “Others”.
HoverA tooltip shows the segment’s name and value; the other segments and legend rows dim. Hovering a legend row highlights its segment.
ProportionsArc sweep = the segment’s share of the total. Keep the ~1.4° gap between segments.
Empty / loadingUse an Empty state or a Skeleton in place of the chart.
Screen readersrole="img" with an aria-label that gives the total and each part.
ColourColour is not the only cue: every segment has a legend row with its name and value.

Source

Values come from the Pie chart — Documentation frames on the “Pie Charts” page of the Cube Core Figma file (Anatomy, Property, Color Annotation, Dimensions, Usage Guidelines, How to use) and the live component sets Pie chart, _Donut and _Pie legend item.

Where Figma disagrees with itself or leaves gaps: there is no API frame, so the behaviour rules on this page are carried over from Bar chart. The Color Annotation gives Monochrome position 5 as Steel Blue/200 and position 6 as Steel Blue/300, and the _Pie legend item swatches follow it; the _Donut always puts Steel Blue/200 last, so with six segments the legend swatches 5 and 6 don’t match the ring — here the swatch always takes its segment’s colour. Usage says “2–6 parts” while the component allows 1 (Ring) – 6. In the Legend items examples the donut stays at five segments while the legend shows three or six rows, against the docs’ own rule; here the segments change with the rows. The “Use a bar chart” Do shows a pie chart in Figma; a bar chart is shown here. The “many tiny slices” Don’t uses the preset six-segment donut; smaller slices are drawn here so the problem is visible. Segment 6 (Accent/Green/600) has the same hex as a positive Delta (Success/600). The Anatomy frame renders no marker 3. The Segments row lists six values but shows five donuts (no 5); all six are shown here.
