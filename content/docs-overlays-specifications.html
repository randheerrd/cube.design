Right Overlay

Stable Last updated Oct 2026

A panel that slides in from the right edge over the page, used for quick, contextual information — drill-downs, details and trends — without navigating away. Built from a Header (breadcrumb, heading, tabs, close), a flexible Body slot, and an optional Footer with actions.

Source

Taken from the Right Overlay documentation in the Copy-of-GC-Design Figma file and the Right overlay library page. Differences from Figma:

  • The Right-Overlay-Header component is 763 wide; here it’s 760, matching the overlay.
  • The Header fill is a raw #FFFFFF and the border is a style (Default Card Stroke) rather than variables — flagged ⚠ in the token tables.
  • The “Quick details” and “Simple detail” mock-ups show a breadcrumb, tabs and an empty body although their captions say “Heading + description, no tabs”. Here they follow the captions.
  • Three Don’t mock-ups (wizard, “Are you sure?”, lone Close button) reuse a generic overlay in Figma; here they show the actual problem.
  • The page scrim isn’t part of the component; the live demo uses Neutrals/Black at 24%.

The code equivalent in Storybook is Overlays / DrawerPanel.
