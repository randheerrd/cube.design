Stepper

Stable Last updated Oct 2026

Shows where the user is in a multi-step process — which steps are done, which is current and what’s next. Two layouts: Stepper-Horizontal (a compact progress bar for short flows) and Stepper-Vertical (a side list with descriptions for longer setup flows). Both are built from Stepper-elements, each with a Stepper-indicator (the circle).

Guidelines Specifications Code (opens Storybook in a new tab) Accessibility (coming soon)

At a glance

Try it: click Next to complete a step, Back to return, or click a completed step to jump back to it. Upcoming steps can’t be clicked.

Horizontal or Vertical?

Pick by the length and complexity of the flow.

Stepper-Horizontal

Short flows: up to 4 steps (max 6), short self-explanatory labels, no descriptions. Acts as a progress indicator — usually at the top of a modal or page.

  2. 2Map all your SKUs
  3. 3Set revenue targets
  4. 4Invite your team

Don’t use Horizontal for long labels — switch to Vertical.

  1. Connect store
  2. Map SKUs
  3. 3Set targets
  4. 4Invite team

Always have exactly one Selected step, in order: Completed → Selected → Upcoming.

Don’t

  2. 2Map SKUs
  3. Set targets
  4. 4Invite team

Don’t mix stages out of order or select two steps.

Writing content

  • Step names — a verb + noun in 1–2 words for horizontal steps (“Map SKUs”, “Invite team”). Vertical steps can be longer.
  • Sentence case, no full stop in names.
  • Descriptions (vertical) — one short sentence about what happens in the step: “Match SKUs to our catalogue.”
  • Awaiting / Locked — the description says why: “Waiting for admin approval.”, “Finish step 2 first.”
  • Compact view — when the steps don’t fit, show “Step 2 of 4 · Map SKUs”.

Behavior & accessibility

TopicWhat to do
Current stepExactly one Selected. Steps before it are Completed; steps after it are Upcoming.
NavigationClick a Completed step to go back. Upcoming and Locked steps aren’t clickable; Awaiting is read-only.
Next / BackButtons outside the stepper, in the page or modal footer. The stepper shows progress.
LockedSet when a condition isn’t met. Explain it in the description (vertical).
SemanticsAn ordered list (<ol>) with aria-current="step" on the Selected step. Completed steps that can be revisited are buttons; announce the stage (“completed”) in text, not only with the icon.
ResponsiveOn small screens the horizontal stepper becomes compact: “Step 2 of 4” + the current name.
