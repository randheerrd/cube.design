Tooltip

Stable Last updated Sep 2026

Two tooltips: a Standard Tooltip for a short line of supplementary text, and a Standard Trendline Tooltip — a small dark data card built from three components (header, table, footer). Everything past that point on this page is an example of the Trendline tooltip's own components assembled for one dashboard situation, not a separate component.

Default placement

A tooltip is positioned near the pointer or the focused anchor, offset just far enough that it never covers the mark, row or region it is describing. It flips to the opposite side of its anchor before it would run past the viewport edge, and keeps a minimum 16px clearance from the bottom of the page at every position.
