Dropdown

Stable Last updated Oct 2026

Lets people choose one or more options from a list that opens on demand. A field (the closed trigger) opens a menu of options, groups, a header and a footer; the Specifications tab lists every value.

Guidelines Specifications Code (not in Storybook yet) Accessibility (coming soon)

At a glance

A Dropdown lets people choose one or more options from a list that opens on demand. The closed trigger (the Dropdown field) opens a Dropdown menu built from options, expandable groups, an optional header and a footer.

1 · Count

More than five options, or little room — use a dropdown. Two to five that fit — show them all as radios or chips.

2 · Selection

Single select closes on click. Multi select adds checkboxes, Select all and an Apply footer.

3 · Shape

Flat list for most data, Nested list for two-level categories, Drilldown for deep trees.

When to use a dropdown

Use a dropdown when there are more than about five options or space is limited. For two to five options that fit on screen, show them all instead.

SituationUse
2 to 5 options, pick oneRadio buttons
2 to 5 options, pick manyCheckboxes or chips
More than 5 options, or limited spaceDropdown
A simple listFlat list
Two-level categoriesNested list — groups expand in place
Deep or long hierarchiesDrilldown list — groups open a second panel

Writing content

  • Field label — a short noun that names the choice: “Platform”, “Period”. Keep it visible unless the context already labels the field.
  • Placeholder — a prompt such as “Select a platform”, never the only label.
  • Options — sentence case, one line, no trailing punctuation. Put the most common option first, or sort alphabetically for long lists.
  • Group names — the category in one or two words: “Snacks”, “Personal care”.
  • Error text — say what to do: “Choose at least one platform”, not “Invalid”.
  • Footer — keep the default actions “Clear” and “Apply”, and the count as “3 selected”.

Behavior & accessibility

TopicWhat to do
Open and closeClick the field, or press Enter, Space or the down arrow, to open. Close on Esc, a click outside, or selecting (single-select).
KeyboardUp and down move between options, Enter selects, and typing searches. Focus moves to Search when it is shown.
Single selectA click selects and closes. The field shows the label and the Value Filled state.
Multi selectA click toggles. Apply commits. Closing without Apply discards changes. The field shows chips, or “+N”.
Select allChecks all visible (filtered) items. The parent shows Intermediate when only some are selected.
PositioningOpen 4px below the field and flip up near the bottom of the screen. The menu is at least as wide as the field.
Empty searchShow “No results”. There is no empty-state variant in the library yet.
