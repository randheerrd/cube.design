Banner & Toast

Two ways to tell people something happened: a full-width Banner for status that stays, and a dark Toast that confirms an action and disappears on its own. The Specifications tab lists every token.

Properties

Designers place a Banner at the top of the screen frame, or a Toast at the top-right, pick the Type and edit the text. In code, a Banner is a persistent page-level alert; a Toast is a transient notification from a toast manager (queue, timer, stack).

Banner

PropertyValue(s)DefaultNotes
TypeError | Warning | Success | Brand | Neutral | WelcomeErrorBanner sentiment. Required.
Show closetrue | falsetrueHide for must-fix issues.
Message / actionsoverrides—⚠ Not TEXT properties — edit the layers. Action button + link only in Error.

Toast

PropertyValue(s)DefaultNotes
TypeToast-success | Toast-warning | Toast-progress | Toast-errorToast-successRequired.
Title / description / actionoverrides—⚠ Not TEXT / BOOLEAN properties — no way to hide the description or action without editing layers.

Behaviour — Banner

PropertyValueNotes
PlacementTop of viewport, full widthPushes content down; never overlaps.
CountOne at a timeShow the most severe; queue the rest.
Dismiss× (if Show close)Remember dismissal per user / per message.
PersistenceUntil resolved or dismissedRe-show when the condition returns.
Semanticsrole="status" (info) / "alert" (error)

Behaviour — Toast

PropertyValueNotes
PlacementTop-right, below headerStack down, 8px gap, max 3; oldest drops off.
Auto-dismisssuccess 4s · warning 6s · error 8s+Progress stays until the task ends.
Timer barAnimates 100% → 0Pause on hover / focus.
ActionOne, optional“View”, “Undo”, “Retry”.
Semanticsrole="status" aria-live="polite" (error: "assertive")Don’t steal focus.
