Components

Bottom sheet

Stable Last updated Oct 2026

A large panel that slides up from the bottom over a dimmed page, for a deep, wide view of something on the page without leaving it. The Specifications tab lists every value.

Guidelines Specifications Code (not in Storybook yet) Accessibility (coming soon)

At a glance

A bottom sheet is a large panel that slides up from the bottom over a dimmed page. Use it for a deep, wide view of something on the page — a full table or a side-by-side analysis — without leaving the page.

Full width

The sheet spans the screen and is about 78% tall, so the top of the page stays visible.

Divided or not

Main content on the left and supporting data on the right — or one wide area.

Step through items

The header’s switcher moves to the previous or next item without closing.

Bottom sheet, right overlay, modal or page?

Use a bottom sheet for a deep, wide view of something the user clicked, while keeping the page behind it.

NeedUseWhy
A deep, wide view of something the user clicked — a full table, a side-by-side analysisBottom sheetFull width gives room to compare; the page stays behind it.
Narrow details about one item, next to the pageRight overlayKeeps most of the page visible.
A short decision or confirmationModal (Popup)Small, centred and quick.
Long, multi-step workA new pageIt needs the whole screen and its own URL.

Writing content

  • Heading — name what the sheet shows: “SKU details”, “Platform split”. Never leave “Heading”.
  • Switcher label — the kind of item, with a colon: “SKU:”, “City:”, “Brand:”. The value is the item’s name.
  • Description — one short line, only when the heading needs context: “Offtake and availability across platforms, MTD.”
  • Content heading — the answer or the item, in a few words; the description adds the key numbers.
  • Tabs — one or two words each: “Trends”, “Details”, “Platforms”.

How to add one in Figma

Add a bottom sheet in five steps.

  1. Place it on the screen
    Duplicate the screen frame and add a 40% black scrim. Then Assets → “Bottom-sheet”, docked to the bottom: X 0, Y = frame height − 800.
  2. Set the header
    Heading = what the sheet shows. Turn Switcher on to move between items (“for ‹ SKU ›”). Description only if needed.
  3. Choose Divided
    Divided on: main content left, supporting data right. Off: one wide area, such as a full table.
  4. Fill the slots
    Left slot: Table-template, Message or your content. Right panel slot: Trends-composition, Table-template or Message. Turn on Tabs on either side for several views.
  5. Annotate behaviour
    Slides up (250ms); closes with ×, Esc or a click on the scrim; content scrolls under a fixed header.

Before you hand off

  • A bottom sheet is the right choice: wide content, and the page context matters.
  • Docked to the bottom over a 40% scrim; the top of the page is visible.
  • The heading is named; the switcher is on if people step through items.
  • Divided only when the left and right are related.
  • Slots are filled with system components (Table-template, Trends, Message).
  • Close, Esc and scrim behaviour is annotated.

Close×, Esc or a click on the scrim. Return focus to the control that opened it.
SwitcherPrevious / next / pick updates the content in place and keeps the selected tab.
ScrollOnly the content area scrolls; the header stays fixed.
FocusTrap focus inside the sheet while it is open. role="dialog" + aria-modal="true", labelled by the heading.
